# Eye Soul

Eye Soul is a web shop selling digital cameras, film cameras and film rolls to customers in Vietnam (Vietnamese UI, prices in VND).

## Catalog

**Product Type**:
One of exactly three kinds of thing the shop sells: Digital Camera, Film Camera, Film Roll. Lenses, accessories and film developing/scanning are not sold (yet).
_Avoid_: Category (when meaning one of these three)

**Digital Camera**:
A new digital camera in one specific configuration (e.g. body only, or with kit lens), sold from stock; one product covers many identical units.
_Avoid_: Máy số, DSLR (as a generic name)

**Film Camera**:
A single used film camera, unique: its own Condition Grade, its own photos, quantity of exactly one. Once sold, it is gone.
_Avoid_: Analog camera, máy cơ, second-hand camera

**Film Roll**:
A new photographic film in one specific format and pack size (e.g. Portra 400, 35mm, 36 exposures), sold from stock as a standalone product, not an accessory of a camera.
_Avoid_: Film, phim (alone — ambiguous with Film Camera)

**Condition Grade**:
The rated state of a Film Camera on a four-step scale (A+, A, B, C), always accompanied by a free-text note on that unit's specific flaws.
_Avoid_: Quality, status

**Product Photo**:
A photo *of* a product (the camera body, the film box).
_Avoid_: Image, thumbnail (as a domain term)

**Sample Photo**:
A photo taken *with* a product: shot by that exact Film Camera unit, by that Digital Camera model, or on that Film Roll. Belongs to exactly one product.
_Avoid_: Film Sample, gallery photo, sample image

**Sold**:
The state of a Film Camera once bought. A Sold Film Camera stays viewable but is no longer offered.
_Avoid_: Out of Stock (for Film Cameras)

**Out of Stock**:
The state of a Digital Camera or Film Roll with no units left; it is still offered, just not purchasable until restocked.
_Avoid_: Sold, sold out

## Shopping

**Cart**:
The products a shopper intends to buy. A shopper can have one before signing in; it merges into their account's Cart on sign-in. Holding a Film Camera in a Cart does not reserve it.
_Avoid_: Basket, bag

## Homepage

**Featured Product**:
A product the shop deliberately promotes on the homepage, within the row for its Product Type.
_Avoid_: Hot product, highlight
