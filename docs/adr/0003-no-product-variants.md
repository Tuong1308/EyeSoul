# No product variants: one product per configuration

Each sellable configuration is its own product with its own price and stock (e.g. "Portra 400 – 35mm – 36 exp" and "Portra 400 – 120" are separate products; a body-only and a kit Digital Camera are separate products), instead of one product with variants. This keeps the catalog, cart and stock model simple and matches Film Cameras, which never have variants. Introducing variants later means migrating products, cart lines and orders, so this is deliberately chosen, not overlooked.
